Our story

Nestled in Hurghada's Touristic Villages on Metro Street, Heart of Styria brings the warmth and flavors of Austria's Styrian province to the Red Sea coast. We are a bakery, restaurant, and kebab house all in one — a place where the Alpine tradition of handcrafted pastries meets Egyptian hospitality.

Every pastry is baked fresh each morning. Every dish is made with care. Whether you are a Hurghada resident or on holiday, we want you to feel completely at home — with hearty breakfasts, original Styrian sweets, satisfying mains, and prices that are genuinely fair.
